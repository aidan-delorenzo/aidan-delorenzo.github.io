Catamount Trail Mapping

In this cartography project, I mapped the Catamount State Forest trail network in western Massachusetts.

Data Sources

The trail vector geometries were gather from a combination of sources: Open Street Maps, Trail Forks, and local knowledge of the site. The hill shade was made from the USGS 10m Digital Elevation Model which was accessed through Google Earth Engine. Finally, the rest of the context (roads, boundaries, and water bodies) was queried from Open Street Maps.
The layout was made in QGIS with labels and some symbology added in Adobe Illustrator.
