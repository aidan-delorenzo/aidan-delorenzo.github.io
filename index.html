About Me

Hello! I'm Aidan DeLorenzo, a GIS analyst with experience in QGIS, ArcGIS, and data science in R. I'm passionate about using mapping and design to better understand and improve the relationship between the built and natural environment.

I hold a joint major in Geography and Architecture from Middlebury College. My geography studies developed my skills in spatial analysis and regional-scale thinking, while architecture gave me a strong foundation in design, the built environment, and challenges such as housing scarcity and building-sector emissions. Together, these disciplines have shaped my approach to problem-solving across scales—from detailed site design to broader geographic systems.

I am Originally from Shelburne Falls, Massachusetts, and when I am away from work I enjoy rock climbing, playing ultimate frisbee, and composing music.
